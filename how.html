How It Works

Netra AI works in the background, continuously analysing your phone’s activity and the latest cyber threat updates.

1
📱

Monitors Your Phone

Looks at messages, calls, notifications and links (with your permission).

2
🌐

Fetches Latest Threats

Uses TinyFish + trusted sources (news, advisory, cyber cells) to learn new scam tactics.

3
🧠

Analyses & Matches

Compares what it finds with known and emerging fraud patterns using AI + rules.

4
⚠️

Warns You

Gives clear, simple alerts with reasons and safe next steps.

5
✅

Optional Reporting

With your consent, shares relevant threat information with cyber authorities (using minimal data).
